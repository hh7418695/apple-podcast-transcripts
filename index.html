Apple Podcast Transcript Viewer

The Apple Podcasts app on iOS and MacOS doesn't allow you to easily export or copy large portions of the transcript. However it's locally stored on MacOS if you view the transcript in the app. This tool allows you to easily copy and extract them.

1Go to the episode in the MacOS Podcasts app and click where it says 'Transcript' (or the triple dots ··· > 'View Transcript').
2Open a Finder window and select 'Go' > 'Go to Folder' (or Cmd+Shift+G) and enter:
~/Library/Group Containers/243LU875E5.groups.com.apple.podcasts
3Drag and drop that entire folder (or its contents) anywhere on this window. The app will automatically find all transcripts inside.

NOTE: All files are locally processed in your browser and never leave your computer.
